## Highlights

ResearchOS 1.0.0 is the first desktop distribution of a research workspace that brings together projects, literature, notes, ideas, experiments, task management, and weekly reviews.

- **Unified research workspace:** keep related work organized without rigid project types.
- **Research records:** manage literature and PDF reading, notes, ideas, experiments and run histories.
- **Planning and reflection:** use Kanban and weekly reviews to follow research progress.
- **Extensibility:** plugin management and configurable AI-assisted workflows.

## Distribution notes

This is the first packaged release. Windows builds have no Authenticode signature. macOS builds use ad-hoc signatures rather than Apple Developer ID signing and are not notarized. Portable builds do not relocate ResearchOS settings or workspace data beside the executable. Check the attached `SHA256SUMS.txt` when downloading.

Cross-platform installation and native-addon compatibility must be checked during the Draft review before publication.
